# Product Principles

## Core Principles
1. Everything serves business outcomes, not AI theater.
2. Anthropomorphic agents are the interaction layer, not the product essence.
3. The underlying model must remain legible as skills, workflows, and orchestration.
4. MVP first: build the smallest closed loop before expanding.
5. Use language a Chinese SME owner can understand at a glance.
6. The interface should immediately answer:
   - what is happening now
   - what needs confirmation
   - what will help the business next

## Product Heuristics
- Prefer “安排谁干什么” over “配置复杂流程”.
- Prefer one reliable loop over five partial modules.
- Prefer operational confidence over visual novelty.
- Prefer decision support plus execution support over dashboard-only reporting.

## Default Trade-offs
- Prefer closure over completeness.
- Prefer understandable structure over theoretical elegance.
- Prefer believable workflow states over fake liveliness.
- Prefer high-signal metrics over dense admin-style control panels.

## UX Tone
- Professional, grounded, and slightly warm.
- Humanized, but never toy-like.
- More “老板的数字员工团队” than “AI companion app”.
